<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

That includes:

- the protocol, deviation log, reports and all documentation (`*.md`)

## What this licence does NOT cover

**Third-party datasets, structures and software retain their own licences and terms. Nothing in
this repository relicenses them, and no third-party licence text is reproduced here as if it were
this project's own.** The table in `THIRD_PARTY.md` names every upstream resource, its version, its
source and its licence as stated by its owner.

In particular:

## Attribution expected in addition to the licence

Citation of the originating depositors' primary publications remains expected practice for every
PDB entry analysed. The cohort manifests list every PDB identifier used, so those citations can be
assembled directly from the deposited record.

## Effect of licence separation

A user who wants only the code takes it under MIT. A user who wants only the derived data or
figures takes them under CC BY 4.0. A user who wants to reproduce the study must additionally
obtain the third-party resources listed in `THIRD_PARTY.md` under their own terms; this repository
provides identifiers, versions, source URLs, checksums and retrieval instructions for each, and
claims ownership of none of them.
